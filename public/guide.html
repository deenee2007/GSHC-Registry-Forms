User Guide: Everyone

Signing in

  1. Open the GSHC Registry address in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. Type your Username and Password, then click Sign In.
  2. The first time, you are asked to choose your own password: at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers. If you received an invitation email, click its link instead and choose your password there.
  3. Forgot your password? Click Forgot password? on the sign in screen and a reset link is emailed to you (valid 60 minutes). If you have no email address on the system, ask the administrator for a one time password.
  4. After 5 wrong passwords the account locks for 15 minutes. You are signed out by itself after a period without use.

The menu

🏠 HomeWhat needs your attention, your units' numbers, Upcoming Court Dates for the next 14 days, quick buttons for each form and your notifications.
📁 Case FileType a suit, case or appeal number (or part of one, such as 45/2026) to see every document for that case, in all units, oldest first.
Your unitUnder Units: your court registry or unit, with its forms and records.
🔑 My AccountChange your password and switch email alerts on or off.
❓ HelpOpens this guide.

You see only the menu items your account is allowed to use. On a phone, the menu opens with the ☰ button at the top left.

Emails you may receive

  • A form is waiting for your signature, or a form you submitted was approved, returned or revoked.
  • Every working day from 7 am: the court dates for the next working day in your court (on Friday, Monday's dates).
  • You can switch these off under 🔑 My Account. The notifications on Home continue either way.

When the internet is down

The system still opens and shows the lists you loaded last. You can fill in a form and click Save Draft: it is kept on your computer, marked Not Synced, and sent by itself when the connection returns (the bottom of the menu shows how many drafts are waiting). Submitting, signing, the case file and reports need the internet.

Keep the system safe

  • Never share your password or signing PIN, not even with the ICT staff.
  • Click Sign Out (bottom of the menu) when you leave a shared computer.
  • Everything you do is recorded in the audit log with your name and the time.

User Guide: Registry and Unit Staff

Making a form

  1. Click your unit under Units in the menu (or a form button on Home).
  2. Choose the form in the list at the top, then click + New Entry.
  3. Type the suit or case number first. If earlier documents exist for that case, a green bar offers to fill in the parties, addresses and court: click Fill them in, then check every box it filled. Dates, fees and amounts are never filled in for you.
  4. Complete the form. Boxes marked * must be filled. Click Save Draft.

To check your work on paper before it is signed, open the record and click 🖨 Print Draft Copy. Draft copies carry a large "DRAFT · NOT VALID" mark.

Sending it for signature

  1. In the list, click Submit for Signature on the record (or open it and use the same button).
  2. Choose who signs each line: the judge of your court, the Registrar, the Chief Registrar and so on. For an appeal panel, tick the judges and choose Any one may approve or All must approve.
  3. If you hold the signing role for a line yourself (for example a Registrar on a hearing notice), you sign it at once with your signing PIN. A form only you sign shows Finalise instead.

Forms signed by hand outside the system (bail bond by the surety, affidavits before the Commissioner for Oaths, notices of appeal by counsel) have no digital signature line: Finalise locks them and gives them their QR code.

After you submit

📤 My SubmissionsYour forms under Approved (signed, ready to print), Awaiting Signature, Returned and Revoked.
ReturnedThe signer wrote why. Open it, read the comments, click Edit, correct it, Save Draft, then submit again.
WithdrawTakes back a form still awaiting signature, so you can change it.
🖨 PrintPrints the signed document exactly as it was signed, with signatures and QR code. Print as many copies as you need.
⬇ Download CopySaves the signed document as a file that opens and prints in any browser, even without internet.

Finding records

  • On your unit page, type in the search box (form, suit number, party or staff name) and use the status buttons and sort list.
  • 📁 Case File shows the whole history of a case. A document's 📁 Case File button opens its case directly. Documents from other units are listed without their contents.
  • A signed document can never be edited. If it is wrong, ask the signer to Revoke it and make a new one.
  • History on a document shows who created, edited, submitted, signed and printed it, and when.

Court dates on Home and in the reminder email come from signed documents. If a hearing is adjourned, the new date appears once a new hearing notice (or other document with the date) is signed.

User Guide: Judges and Signers

This page is for everyone who signs: judges, the Chief Registrar, Registrars, the Chief Bailiff, the Deputy Sheriff, the Director of Litigation and Commissioners for Oaths.

Setting up once: 🖊 My Signature

  1. Click 🖊 My Signature in the menu.
  2. Draw your signature with the mouse or your finger, or choose Upload a scan (a clear photo or scan on white paper, PNG or JPEG, under 1 MB). Click Save Signature.
  3. Under Signing PIN, type your Account password, a New PIN and Repeat PIN, then click Set PIN.

You can change your signature later. Documents you signed before keep the signature they were signed with.

Signing

  1. Click ✍ Awaiting My Signature (the number shows how many forms wait). You also get an email for each.
  2. Click Review to read the document exactly as it will print.
  3. If it is correct, click ✔ Approve & Sign and type your signing PIN. Your signature and the date are placed on the document. When every line is signed, the document is final, gets its QR code and goes back to the person who submitted it.
  4. If something is wrong, click Return with Comments and say what must change. The form goes back to the submitter to correct.
Appeal panelsThe submitter chooses whether any one judge or all judges on the panel must sign. The document shows who has signed so far.
Wrong PINAfter 5 wrong PINs, signing locks for 15 minutes. Forgotten PIN: the administrator clears it (Reset PIN) and you set a new one under 🖊 My Signature.
RevokeIf a document you signed was issued in error, open it and click Revoke, giving the reason. It is never deleted, but anyone who scans its QR code is told it is revoked and must not be relied on.

Please remember

  • Your PIN is your signature. Never tell it to anyone or write it near the computer.
  • Read the whole document before you sign: once signed it cannot be changed, only revoked.
  • If you use a shared computer, sign out when you finish.

User Guide: Execution / Bailiffs Unit

You make, submit and print forms the same way as registry staff (see that page). These points are particular to the Execution / Bailiffs Unit.

Who signs your forms (unless the administrator changes it)

WritsWrits of attachment and sale, writs of possession and the writ of attachment and sale of goods: the Judge.
LettersStatutory notice for public auction, requests for police or NSCDC personnel, notice to vacate: the Chief Bailiff, for the Deputy Sheriff.
Notice of AttachmentThe Chief Registrar.
Public Auction ReceiptThe Chief Bailiff, Deputy Sheriff, Director of Litigation and Chief Registrar.
By handAffidavit of service (before the Commissioner for Oaths) and judgment summons (by the judgment creditor or counsel).

Filling the execution forms

  • Writs of possession: choose how the parties are described (Judgment Creditor / Judgment Debtor, or Plaintiff / Defendant) and who the court gave possession to. The writ then names the parties the same way throughout.
  • Suit Number: fill it on every writ, letter and receipt, even where it is optional. It links the document to its case in 📁 Case File.
  • Requests for personnel: the number of personnel is optional; leave it empty to print "REQUEST FOR PERSONNEL".
  • Statutory notice: choose movable or immovable property and the number of days given.
  • Notice of attachment: the daily fee for keeping possession prints as you type it.
  • Writ of attachment and sale of goods: type the judicial division's name only (for example Gombe); "Judicial Division" is added for you.

Before enforcing, check 📁 Case File for the suit number: it shows every order, notice and writ issued in the case and whether any was revoked.

User Guide: Administrators

⚙ Settings

Users+ Add User: name, username, email, units, privileges (view, create, edit, finalize, delete, print, reports) and signing roles. With an email address, tick the invitation box so the person chooses their own password; otherwise copy the one time password shown and give it to them privately. On each user: Edit, Email Reset Link, One Time Password or Reset Password, Reset PIN, and Deactivate (documents they signed stay valid).
Units+ Add Unit adds another Court Registry (numbered for you, with the registry forms) or an Other Unit (Probate, Accounts and so on), which starts with no forms.
Form AccessWhich forms each unit may use.
Signing RulesWho signs each form. A form with no signature line is signed by hand outside the system.
Audit LogEvery sign in, edit, submission, signature, print, revocation and backup, with the user and time.

Give every judge and signer the right signing role and assign them to their court, then ask them to set up 🖊 My Signature. A form cannot be submitted to a role nobody holds.

Home, reports and alerts

  • Home shows the whole system: users, signers, units, forms awaiting signature, signed documents, every court's upcoming dates, and Backups.
  • 📊 Reports: activity for any period, form, status, signer and court, with 🖨 Print and ⬇ Download for Excel. Staff with the reports privilege see only their own unit.
  • Email alerts and reminders can be switched off for one user (Edit) or for everybody (Settings, Users).

Backups

  • A backup is made every day by itself and kept 30 days, privately on Cloudinary. Signed copies are backed up as well.
  • On Home, under Backups: the last backup, the list with ⬇ Download, and ⟳ Back up now. Make sure your own account has an email address: if a backup fails, administrators are emailed.
  • Restoring a backup into a new database is done by the ICT staff; the steps are in the system's technical notes (README).

Checking a Document

For anyone handed a court document: police, prisons, banks, counsel and members of the public. No account is needed.

  1. Scan the QR code at the bottom of the document with a phone camera, or open the /verify page of the GSHC Registry address and type the verification code printed beside the QR code (it looks like GSHC-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX).
  2. Read the answer:
Genuine documentIssued and signed through the GSHC Registry system and not altered. Check that the form, reference, court and signers shown match the paper in your hand.
RevokedIssued by the court but since revoked. It must not be relied on.
Record alteredThe stored record no longer matches what was signed. Contact the court registry.
Not foundNo signed document has this code. Check the code; otherwise treat the document as not genuine.

The check shows the form, reference, court and who signed it, but never the document's contents. A document marked "DRAFT · NOT VALID" or "AWAITING SIGNATURE · NOT VALID" is not an official document.
