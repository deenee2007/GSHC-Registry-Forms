Process Registry Clerk

Your work: file new cases, give the filer the invoice, record the receipt, send the filing to the Process Registrar, then print the slip and dispatch the file once the Hon. Chief Judge assigns the case.

Once, before you start

  1. Sign in with your email and the password given to you; choose your own password when asked.
  2. Open Process Registry in the menu. You see the tabs Filings, Summary and Fees List.

Filing a new case

  1. Click + New Filing. Choose the case type, type both parties, the nature of the case, the originating process, counsel and the date of filing. Add the parties' addresses and email addresses if given.
  2. Pick the fees from the list; the total is worked out. For a case filed by the State, or an EFCC matter, tick Official (no fee). Click Save.
  3. The filing opens. Click Print Invoice and give it to the filer to pay at revenue.
  4. When the filer returns with the receipt: open the filing, click Edit, type the receipt number and choose the photo or PDF of the receipt, then Save. A receipt number already used on another filing is refused.
  5. Attach the scanned papers (writ, statement of claim, affidavits) with + Add Document under Documents.
  6. Click Send to Process Registrar. A filing that comes back Returned shows the reason: correct it and send it again.

After the case is assigned

  1. Choose Assigned (slip and dispatch) in the status list. Click Print Slips, tick the cases and print; each slip prints on its own A6 card. Fix each slip to its file.
  2. When the files leave, click Dispatch Files, tick the files going now and click Dispatch. Each court is told and can then mark the file received.
  3. A case marked "reassigned from" another court: collect the file from that court, print the new slip and dispatch it to the new court.

At the end of the day, open the Fees List tab to check every payment has its receipt number, for reconciling with revenue.

Practice

  • File a practice civil case with two fees, print its invoice and record a receipt.
  • Add one scanned document and send the filing to the Process Registrar.
  • After the trainer assigns it, print its slip and dispatch the file.

Process Registrar

Your work: check each filing and its receipt, register it with the next suit number, or return it with comments. Each registered case goes to the Hon. Chief Judge with the court next in turn proposed.

Once, before you start

  1. Sign in and choose your own password.
  2. Set your signature and PIN under My Signature if the administrator asks you to sign documents.

Registering filings

  1. The number beside Process Registry in the menu shows filings waiting for you. Open Process Registry; the list shows With Process Registrar.
  2. Click Open on a filing. Check the parties, the type of case, the fees and the receipt number. Click View receipt to see the uploaded receipt.
  3. If all is correct, click Register and Give Number. The case gets the next number of its letter (for example GM/12/2026, GM/12c/2026) and goes to the Hon. Chief Judge.
  4. If something is wrong, click Return with Comments and say exactly what to correct. The filing goes back to the clerk.
  5. You may file a case yourself with + New Filing; open it and register it straight away.

Good to know

  • One receipt pays for one filing. Where one receipt truly covers two filings, the system asks you to confirm; your confirmation is recorded.
  • The Summary tab gives the cases registered by type, official cases, fees recorded and cases assigned per court for any period. The Fees List tab lists every payment with its invoice and receipt numbers.
  • You can see the suit numbers given this year; only the administrator sets where they continue from.

Practice

  • Open a practice filing, view its receipt and register it. Note the suit number given.
  • Return another practice filing with a comment; check the clerk sees it as Returned.
  • Open the Summary tab for this month.

Hon. Chief Judge

Your work: assign each registered case to a court, act on requests to reassign, reassign cases when a court cannot continue, and follow the work of the courts.

Once, before you start

  1. Sign in and choose your own password.
  2. Open My Signature: Draw your signature (or Upload a scan), click Save Signature, then set your signing PIN with Set PIN. Your signature goes on every assignment slip. Never share your PIN.

Assigning new cases

  1. The number beside Awaiting Assignment in the menu shows cases waiting. Open it.
  2. Click Review & Assign on a case. The court next in turn is already chosen; keep it or choose another.
  3. Type your signing PIN and click Assign. The Process Registry prints the slip and sends the file; the court is told.

Reassigning

  1. Requests to Reassign lists cases a court has asked to move, with the reason. Click Reassign, choose the court, keep or change the reason, type your PIN and confirm; or click Decline with a comment.
  2. To move many cases (a Judge elevated, retired or away), open the Reassign tab and choose the court the cases are in. Tick the cases and choose a court for each, or send them all to one court with Apply, or tick the courts to share them and click Spread. Then click Reassign the ticked cases, choose the reason and type your PIN.
  3. The suit number never changes. Each case gets a new slip; the file goes back through the Process Registry to the new court.

Following the courts

  • Court Statistics: cases assigned, disposed of and pending per court for any period; Pending cases lists a court's oldest cases first. Print or download for Excel.
  • Fees List: every payment in the Process Registry and the courts for a day or a period.

Practice

  • Set your signature and PIN.
  • Assign a practice case to the court proposed, and another to a different court.
  • Reassign a practice case and decline a practice request.

Court Registry Staff

Your work: receive the files of new cases, keep the Case Register and the Court Diary, prepare the court's forms for signing, and file processes brought later.

New cases and the register

  1. New cases assigned to your court show on Home under New Cases Assigned and as a number beside Case Register.
  2. When the file arrives from the Process Registry, open Case Register and click File received on the case. It works only after the Process Registry has dispatched the file.
  3. Click View under a suit number to see the whole case and its documents. Use + Add Document for papers filed later.
  4. When the case ends, click Record Remark, choose the remark and the date of disposal.
  5. A process filed later (a motion): in View, click File a Process, choose the process and the fees, save, then Print Invoice; record the receipt when it comes back.
  6. To ask the Hon. Chief Judge to move a case, click Request Reassignment in View and give the reason.

The Court Diary

  1. Open Court Diary. Click + Fix a Date, choose the case, the date, the time and what it is coming up for.
  2. After the sitting, click Record Outcome (also on Home under Today in Court). If adjourned, give the next date: it goes into the diary by itself.
  3. For the day's list, click Draw Cause List, arrange the cases, then Open Cause List and send it for signing.

Forms

  1. Open your court under Units, choose the form and click + New Entry; or use Fill a Form in the case's View to start with the case details filled.
  2. Check every box, click Save Draft, then Submit for Signature and choose the judge. Signed forms are under My Submissions, ready to print.

Practice

  • Mark a practice file received and fix its first date in the diary.
  • Record an outcome with an adjournment; draw and open the cause list.
  • Fill a hearing notice from a case and submit it for signature.

Judge

Your work: read and sign the forms of your court with your signing PIN, or return them with comments; follow your day's cases and the court's register.

Once, before you start

  1. Sign in and choose your own password.
  2. Open My Signature. Draw your signature with the mouse or your finger, or Upload a scan on white paper, and click Save Signature.
  3. Under Signing PIN, type your Account password, a New PIN and Repeat PIN, then click Set PIN. Your PIN is your signature: never share it.

Signing

  1. The number beside Awaiting My Signature shows forms waiting; you also get an email for each.
  2. Click Review and read the whole document as it will print.
  3. If it is right, click Approve & Sign and type your PIN. If not, click Return with Comments and say what must change.
  4. Signed by Me and Returned by Me keep every form you have dealt with.
  5. A signed document cannot be changed. If one was issued in error, open it and click Revoke with the reason.

Your court

  • Home shows Today in Court: the day's cases, with Record Outcome on each.
  • Court Diary shows the dates of your court by day, week or month.
  • Case Register shows the cases of your court; View opens a case with its documents.

After 5 wrong PINs, signing locks for 15 minutes. A forgotten PIN is cleared by the administrator, then you set a new one.

Practice

  • Set your signature and PIN.
  • Sign one practice form and return another with a comment.
  • Find the signed form under Signed by Me.

Execution / Bailiffs Unit

Your work: prepare writs, notices, letters and receipts for enforcing judgments, send them for signing, and print the signed copies.

Making a form

  1. Open Execution / Bailiffs Unit under Units, choose the form and click + New Entry.
  2. Type the Suit Number first, on every writ, letter and receipt: it links the document to its case. Use Choose a case at the top to fill the case details, or Fill them in when the green bar offers details from earlier documents. Check every box it filled.
  3. Complete the form and click Save Draft. Use Print Draft Copy to check it on paper.
  4. Click Submit for Signature and choose the signers. A form signed by hand outside the system shows Finalise instead.
  5. When signed, it is under My Submissions, Approved: open it and Print.

Who signs (unless the administrator changes it)

WritsThe Judge.
Statutory notice, police or NSCDC requests, notice to vacateThe Chief Bailiff, for the Deputy Sheriff.
Notice of AttachmentThe Chief Registrar.
Public Auction ReceiptThe Chief Bailiff, the Deputy Sheriff and the Chief Registrar.
Affidavit of service, judgment summonsSigned by hand; use Finalise.

Good to know

  • Before enforcing, check Case File for the suit number: it shows every order, notice and writ in the case and whether any was revoked.
  • A form Returned by a signer shows why: open it, click Edit, correct it and submit again.

Practice

  • Make a practice writ of possession for a case and submit it to the judge.
  • Make a statutory notice for public auction and submit it to the Chief Bailiff.
  • Look up the practice case in Case File.

Administrator

Your work: set up users and their access, keep the settings right, help users who are locked out, and watch the backups and the audit log.

Users

  1. Open Settings, tab Users, and click + Add User. Give the name, title, email, units, privileges and signing roles (Judge, Registrar, Process Registrar, Hon. Chief Judge and so on). Tick the box to email an invitation.
  2. On a user: Edit; Email Reset Link or One Time Password for a forgotten password; Reset PIN for a forgotten signing PIN; Deactivate when someone leaves (what they signed stays valid).

Settings to keep right

Units, Form Access, Signing RulesThe courts and units, the forms each may use, and who signs each form.
Process RegistryThe suit number prefix, the case types and their letters, Suit numbers this year (set once where cases were numbered by hand), the fees, the payment note on the invoice, whether the receipt upload is required, emails to parties, the diary business list and the courts that hear EFCC matters.
Audit LogWho did what and when: sign ins, edits, signings, assignments and changes to settings.

Every week

  • On Home, check that the daily backup ran; click Back up now before any big change.
  • Look through the Audit Log for anything unusual.
  • Use Reports and Download for Excel for returns.

Before going live, Clear Test Data on Home removes the practice forms and cases so they do not pass as genuine. Do it once, after training, and only on the trainer's instruction.

Practice

  • Add a practice user with an invitation, then deactivate it.
  • Give a judge the Judge signing role for one court; reset a PIN.
  • Set the last suit number used for the criminal letter; run Back up now.
